## [0.1.0]

### Added
- Live device list with signal strength, type, address and state.
- Connect, disconnect, pair, trust and remove actions.
- Adapter controls: power, scan, discoverable.
- Details, adapter and activity-log panels.
- Three built-in themes (Midnight, Nord, Ember).
- Built-in pairing agent for "just works" and confirmation pairing.
